Platform Manager

PM هو مركز التحكمPM is the control hub

Platform Manager يحتفظ بالتكوين والأحداث والترخيص ويدير ARM وJob Manager. في البيئات الكبيرة يفضل أن يكون مخصصاً.Platform Manager stores configuration, events, licensing, and runs ARM and Job Manager. In larger deployments, it is typically dedicated.

ARM

Alarming and Response

  • Processes alarm rules and notification actions.
  • Use scarm.log when alarms or emails behave unexpectedly.
  • Validate recipients, policies, SMTP, and SmartResponse configuration.
Job Manager

Scheduled work

  • Runs scheduled reports and background jobs.
  • Use lrjobmgr.log when reports fail or appear empty.
  • Confirm data exists before assuming the report is broken.
