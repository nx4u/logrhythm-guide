Methodology

منهجية التروبلشوتنقTroubleshooting Methodology

1

Define

Make a clear problem statement: what is missing, delayed, failing, or noisy?

2

Gather

Collect timeline, scope, component status, service logs, counters, and recent changes.

3

Analyze

Map evidence to the Life of a Log and identify the likely failing stage.

4

Hypothesize

Write one likely cause, such as permissions, firewall, parsing, workload, or ARM notification conflict.

5

Test

Change one thing, validate outcome, then roll back or continue.

6

Document

Record symptoms, root cause, evidence, fix, and prevention.
